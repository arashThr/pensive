Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

Overview

Pensive is a personal knowledge indexing service that helps you save, organize, and search content you've read online. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web application and browser extension.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Password (encrypted and stored securely)
  • Subscription status and billing information (processed through Stripe)

Content You Save

  • URLs of web pages you bookmark
  • Page titles and metadata
  • Extracted text content from saved pages
  • Clean HTML content (with ads and tracking removed)
  • Generated summaries and tags (premium feature using Google Gemini)

Technical Information

  • Authentication tokens for browser extension
  • Session data for maintaining login state
  • Basic analytics (page views, feature usage) via Umami Analytics

How We Use Your Information

  • Core Service: Store and index your saved content for personal searchability
  • Content Enhancement: Generate summaries and tags using AI (available for all users)
  • Account Management: Authenticate users and manage subscriptions
  • Service Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve features
  • Communication: Send important service updates (rare, no marketing)

Browser Extension Privacy

Our browser extension only processes data when you explicitly save a page by clicking the extension icon.

Content Extraction Methods

  • Full Page Content Capture (Default): The extension extracts the main article. Needed for sites like Reddit and Stack Overflow.
  • Server-Side Capture Only: Extension sends only the URL; server fetches the content. Won't work on paywalled sites.
  • Only accesses the current tab when you click "save"
  • Never tracks your browsing or collects data from pages you don't save
  • Stores only your authentication token and capture method preference locally

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Service Providers: Google Gemini for AI features
  • Payment Processing: Stripe for subscription billing
  • Analytics: Umami Analytics (privacy-focused, no personal data)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law

Data Management and Your Rights

You have full control over your data through your account settings:

  • Export: Download your saved content in standard formats
  • Delete All Content: Permanently removes all bookmarks while keeping your account
  • Delete Account: Permanently deletes your entire account and all data

Beta Service Notice

Important: Pensive is currently in beta. While we strive to protect your data, there is a possibility that data may be lost or wiped during updates, migrations, or unexpected issues. We strongly recommend regularly exporting your bookmarks.

Legal Jurisdiction and GDPR

Pensive is operated from Stockholm, Sweden, governed by Swedish data protection laws and GDPR. Under GDPR, EU/EEA users have the right to access, correct, export, and delete their data.
